Stark sells 14 offline apps, and 13 of them are paid once with no subscription

The small software company's catalog covers scam defense, bookkeeping, contractor management, teaching and video editing. The apps need no account and are built to run without an internet connection.

2026-11-20. Stark, a small software company, is offering 14 software products built around three choices: pay once, work offline, and keep your data on your own device. Thirteen of the 14 are sold for a one-time payment, several with a free edition. The exception is stated plainly on Stark's own pages: Stark Quill, a novel-writing app, is free to use, and its Pro features are sold by the year.

Most of the apps run in a web browser from a downloaded folder and keep their data in that browser on the user's computer; a few are desktop programs for Windows, macOS or Linux. None require an account, and Stark's website says the products carry no ads or trackers. Stark Ledger, Stark Family Vault and Stark Trades encrypt their data with a passphrase only the user knows, and Stark Warden's vault is encrypted the same way. The trade-off is stated in their own documentation: if the passphrase is lost, nobody can recover the data, so the apps ask users to make backups.

Languages

The Stark website is available in seven languages, including Spanish. Language support inside the apps varies and is listed on each product page: Stark Trades is fully bilingual in English and Spanish, and Stark Warden's key screens have a Spanish machine translation marked as pending review. Some apps, such as Stark Math and Stark Career, are English only.

Limits, stated plainly

Each product page includes an "honest limits" section. Examples: Stark Ledger and Stark Trades are not tax, legal or payroll advice; Stark Warden is general information, not legal advice; Stark Covenant is not certified as an EHR or medical device; Stark Builder does not size structures or check building codes. Offline, single-device software also means no cloud sync between computers, and data kept in a browser can be erased if the browser's site data is cleared, which is why the apps include backup tools. Stark does not publish user counts or reviews yet.

Availability

The products are sold on itch.io and listed at https://starkforall.com. Most run in a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari browser and work offline once downloaded.
