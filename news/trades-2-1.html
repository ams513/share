Stark Trades 2.1 gives small contractors subcontractor, bid and lien-waiver tools in English and Spanish, with no subscription

The offline app runs a trade business from one office computer and field phones, encrypts its data with the owner's passphrase, and costs $29 once.

2026-11-20. Stark today released Stark Trades 2.1, an offline tool for small contractors, subcontractors and specialty trades that need to track subcontractors, bids, compliance, payments, crews and permits. Every screen, template and printed document works in English and Spanish, so an office that writes in English and a crew that reads in Spanish can use the same records. Stark Trades costs $29 for one user, paid once, or $149 once for a Pro business licence covering up to 10 devices. There is no subscription and no account.

Stark built Trades around the paperwork that tends to fall on the owner of a small shop: whose insurance has lapsed, which bid left out what, which lien waiver is still missing before a payment goes out, and which job is losing money.

Subcontractors and compliance

  • A sub bench with scorecards and preferred, backup and do-not-use lists, plus onboarding packets and checklists.
  • License and insurance tracking with red, amber and green status. Assigning a sub whose license or insurance is expired or missing triggers a hard warning, and the override reason is kept on record.
  • Bid invitations and bid leveling that prices exclusions in, with one-click award.
  • A lien waiver gate on sub payments, retainage, sub invoice approval, backcharges with photo evidence, and callbacks charged back to the sub who caused them.
  • A 1099-NEC prep list with amounts only; no tax ID numbers are stored.

Jobs, money and field work

  • Estimates with an editable price book, change orders with sign-off, schedule-of-values billing, job costing and a markup and margin calculator.
  • Sequenced schedules with a Gantt view, inspection booking reminders, delay ripple warnings and a capacity forecast that names the bottleneck trade.
  • A field mode for phones: daily job sheet, punch list with photos, time in and out, daily log, and an encrypted handoff file between the office and the field.

New in 2.1

  • Service agreements and maintenance plans, recurring jobs, and a quote-to-cash pipeline.
  • Van and shop stock with par levels, and purchase orders whose cost goes to the job.
  • Weekly timesheets with approval, crew certifications with 30, 14 and 7-day alerts, and a warning before sending someone without a required certification.
  • A site safety checklist and ten toolbox talks in English and Spanish, with attendance and a printable sign-in sheet.
  • Permits grouped by city or county, with average days to issue and expiry alerts.
  • Seven trade presets: plumbing, electrical, HVAC, general contracting, roofing, landscaping and irrigation, and remodeling and carpentry.

Privacy

Data is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from the owner's passphrase (PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 rounds). The app contains no network code, no accounts and no analytics. Every export passes through a redaction step with a preview, and the app refuses to save full Social Security numbers, tax ID digits, bank account or routing numbers, or card numbers.

Limits, stated plainly

Stark Trades is not legal, tax, payroll or accounting software and does not give legal or tax advice. Agreement, change order, waiver and backcharge texts are templates for a lawyer to review, and the Spanish templates are translations that need the same review. Lien law, licensing, permit and insurance rules differ by state and city. Safety checklists and toolbox talks are general reminders, not a replacement for OSHA rules, a written safety program or a competent person on site. The app cannot verify a license, certification or permit; status comes from the dates entered. It is offline and single-computer by design: there is no cloud sync, customer portal, text, email, card payment or GPS. If the passphrase is lost, nobody can recover the data.

Price and availability

Stark Trades 2.1 is available now on itch.io: https://ams513.itch.io/stark-trades. Standard (one user) is $29, paid once. Pro (up to 10 devices) is $149, paid once. It runs in a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari browser in a normal window and works offline once downloaded. A fictional demo company is loaded on first launch and can be cleared in Settings.
